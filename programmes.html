Our Programmes

Three sections, one approach: a blended curriculum that puts the learner at the centre.

Day Care

From 6 months

A safe, loving and stimulating environment for our youngest learners. We combine rest, play and early learning activities that support every child's natural curiosity.

  • Open Monday to Friday, 7:30am to 4:30pm
  • Rest after lunch, then selected learning activities
  • Pick-up by 5:00pm
  • Three balanced meals a day
Kindergarten

From 3 years

Play-based learning that builds strong foundations in language, numbers and creativity. Our computer lab adds a modern, hands-on dimension to every child's day.

  • 7:00am to 12:30pm
  • Play, language, number work and creative skills
  • Tablets with learning apps in our computer lab
  • Clubs and sports, including swimming (optional)
Lower Primary

P.1 – P.3 and above, from 6 years

A learner-centred primary experience that blends the national curriculum with ICT, e-learning and hands-on discovery. Day and boarding options available.

  • 7:30am to 4:30pm
  • Numeracy and English tested at admission in some cases
  • Day or boarding
  • Library, e-learning and research support

Curriculum Highlights

Every programme at Dugnad is built around four pillars that prepare children for life — not just exams.

ICT & E-Learning

Tablets, headsets and over 200 educational apps for literacy, numeracy and logic.

Creative Arts

Music, dance, drama, art and design build confidence and self-expression.

Sports & Clubs

Scouts, chess, cookery and swimming keep body and mind active and healthy.

Balanced Meals

Break tea, lunch and evening tea support physical and mental growth every day.
