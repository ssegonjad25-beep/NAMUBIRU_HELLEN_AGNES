Begin your admission journey

We are delighted that you are considering Dugnad School for your child. Our admissions process is simple, friendly and designed to help every family feel at home from the very first visit.

Whether your child is joining our Day Care, Kindergarten or Lower Primary, our team will guide you through every step.

Send an Enquiry

Four simple steps

From the first enquiry to the first day of school.

Get a form

Collect an application form from the Head teacher's office, or contact the administration.

Bring documents

2 passport photos of the child, 2 of the parents, a copy of the birth certificate and the immunisation card.

Child assessment

For primary entry, a short numeracy and English test shows which class fits.

Pay and start

Pay the UGX 50,000 admission fee and the child is admitted.

What to bring

Please prepare these documents before visiting the school office:

  • 2 passport photos of the child
  • 2 passport photos of each parent or guardian
  • A copy of the child's birth certificate
  • A copy of the child's immunisation card

Admission Fee

UGX 50,000

Paid once by every new entrant.

Frequently asked questions

When can my child start?

We accept new learners throughout the term where space allows. Contact the office to confirm availability.

Do you offer boarding?

Yes. Boarding is available for Lower Primary learners, with full-time care and balanced meals.

Is transport available?

Yes. Our school van picks up and drops off learners at rates based on distance and stage.

What age can my child join?

From 6 months for Day Care, from 3 years for Kindergarten and from 6 years for Lower Primary.

Do you provide meals?

Yes. Break tea, lunch and evening tea are prepared fresh each day for Day Care and Primary learners.

How safe is the school?

Very safe. We have a fenced compound, 24-hour CCTV and a strict pick-up policy for every child.

Still have questions?

Our team is here to help you every step of the way.
