Why Choose Dugnad?

Because every child deserves more than a classroom — they deserve a community.

An education that lasts a lifetime

At Dugnad, we believe that the best learning happens by doing. Our hands-on approach, low teacher-to-pupil ratio, and warm community culture give every child the confidence to explore, question and grow.

Parents choose us because we combine the warmth of a family with the standards of a modern school. From the first day in Day Care to the last day of Primary, your child is known, valued and challenged.

What sets us apart

Nine reasons families across Wakiso choose Dugnad School.

Blended Curriculum

A rich mix of the national curriculum, ICT and hands-on discovery that keeps children curious.

Learner-Centred

Small classes and a low teacher-to-pupil ratio give each child personal attention every day.

ICT & E-Learning

Tablets, headsets and over 200 educational apps prepare children for a digital future.

Safety First

24-hour CCTV, a fenced compound and a strict pick-up policy keep every child secure.

Three Balanced Meals

Break tea, lunch and evening tea are prepared fresh to support healthy growth.

Clean Environment

A tidy, well-ventilated campus where children can learn and play safely.

School Van Transport

Reliable pick-up and drop-off at rates based on distance and stage.

Qualified Educators

Passionate, experienced teachers who care about each child's progress.

Inclusive Community

Children of every race, tribe and religion learn together as brothers and sisters.
