About Dugnad School

A contemporary, fast-growing school where every child is unique.

A school where every child is unique

Dugnad is a contemporary, fast-growing kindergarten and day care in Kavule-Jomayi Estate, along Matuga–Semuto Road, Wakiso District.

We build a whole child through formal and informal early childhood learning. Our talented, experienced staff keep a low teacher-to-pupil ratio, so every learner gets the attention they deserve.

Children of every race, ethnicity, tribe and religion learn here as brothers and sisters. We do not tolerate segregation of any kind.

Our Core Values

  • Teamwork
  • Integrity
  • Respect
  • God fearing
  • Diversity

Our Mission

To inspire and empower an individual with sustainable skills and knowledge for self-reliance within society.

Our Vision

A self-reliant, co-operative and prosperous citizen.

Our Philosophy

To voluntarily work together for a common goal. The name Dugnad comes from this Norwegian idea of collective effort.

Our Motto

We Learn by Doing — a hands-on approach that makes learning last a lifetime.
